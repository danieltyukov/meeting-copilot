Welcome to Sparky

Sparky transcribes the call in the active tab and drafts what you could say next: an answer when you are asked something, talking points otherwise.

  • Only use it where you have consent to transcribe the call.
  • Audio is sent to Deepgram; prompts go to the Anthropic API (your keys).
  • It reads the participant names the call page shows, to label voices. Nothing else on the page is read.

Shown once. Settings can bring it back.
